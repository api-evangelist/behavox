# Behavox

> Behavox builds AI-native compliance and conduct-surveillance software that helps the world’s leading financial institutions detect risk, misconduct, and market abuse across every communication channel.

Behavox combines purpose-built AI models trained on financial-markets language with a unified data platform spanning surveillance, archiving, investigation, and supervision.
